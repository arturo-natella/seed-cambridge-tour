    About this model
    This Cambridge concept keeps the six-site, 500 MW limit. Each person receives eight active coding hours daily on the top model. Token intensity, overlapping usage and hardware performance determine how many people fit; those assumptions are explained inside AI & energy. The parcel is illustrative; land and grid access remain to be confirmed.
